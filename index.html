About Me

I'm a Research Engineer at InteraXon Inc. (Muse) in Toronto. I have a PhD in Neurophysiology, and most of my work sits where that research meets software.

I was previously co-founder and CTO of Helment. I also work as a freelance developer under the name softcoding for you, for clients such as Acupuntura Tung and Off Earth Games, and publish some of my own projects as open source.
